this is what each length delivers as the game currently ships.

  before the retune   15% 10% 15%    28% 18% 28%    28% 21% 31%

Epic did not move — not in share and not in composition. The regression gate is
stronger than the percentages: the epic record ID SET is identical, id for id,
on all three seeds, because v6Span's denominator is the same CFG span the
numbers were tuned against, so epic is 1.0 by construction.

Short gains exactly what the thresholds were hiding: balanced, centenary,
longReign, veteran (and v8story on the seed whose press streak reaches two).
The century gains longReign. Nothing is lost anywhere.

Two figures in this table are floors on the game, not measures of a player.
The harness takes the first choice on offer, so it loses government early and
never returns: it calls no referendum and signs no treaty at any length, and 27
of the 39 records are never earned in any run. Whether a competent player at
fifty years reaches the state-gated records is NOT measured here and is not
measurable with this harness.

--- the previous (pre-retune) baseline, kept for comparison -------------------

tools/pacing.js used to report `V6_ACHIEVEMENTS.filter(a => a.test(st)).length`
— it re-ran every test against the FINAL state and counted what happened to be
true at that instant. The game does not work that way: v6AchievementsTick
latches, and the game's own displays (the end card, the persisted hall entry)
count the latched map. That instrument bug was fixed in S8b (PR #13); the
figures published in PR #10 (short 8%, century 18-23%, epic 21-28%) measured
the wrong quantity and are superseded by the "before the retune" row above.

RE-BASELINED AFTER S9f — THE FOUR-RUNG LADDER
================================================================================

Every statute now has four rungs where it had between one and four. The rows
are parity-exact at every position an old save, seed, want or programme target
could occupy (tools/roads.js asserts this, statute by statute and channel by
channel), but the AI does not legislate to old positions: its wants were
rescaled with everything else, a full build is four bills instead of two, and
the per-rung capital price moved to match. The trajectories below are therefore
DIFFERENT ON PURPOSE. That is the order being carried out, not a regression.

What the tool gates on is unchanged and still holds: every length option plays
to its end year and delivers a complete arc.
